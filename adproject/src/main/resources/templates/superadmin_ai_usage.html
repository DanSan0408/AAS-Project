About these figures

  • Cost is an estimate: tokens times the provider's published price, with the cheaper rate for cached input. DeepSeek charges double at peak (01:00-04:00 and 06:00-10:00 UTC on weekdays); Chinese public holidays are counted as peak, so the estimate can be a little high. The balance at the top is the real figure.
  • Only numbers are stored: who asked, which feature, tokens, time and the kind of failure. Prompts, comments, names and answers are never kept.
  • Figures start from the day this page was first deployed, and records older than 400 days are deleted.
  • Requests answered from memory (the same question within ten minutes) and requests refused by the per-person limit cost nothing; they are counted under Saved.
