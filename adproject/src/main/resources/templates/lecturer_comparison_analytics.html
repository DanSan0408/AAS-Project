How to read these comparisons

Only lecturer evaluations marked Final or Submitted are included; drafts and student peer/self evaluations are excluded. Scores are divided by the highest configured rating for each criterion and expressed as percentages (the stored criterion maximum is used for legacy criteria without ratings). These are comparisons of awarded ratings, before final grade or factor adjustments. Each target score uses the marks available for its scored criteria; missing criteria are not treated as zero.

Group evaluations copied to team members count once per lecturer, team and criterion. Team filters use current team membership. Lecturer averages describe the selected work, so differences can also reflect different teams or rubric assignments. Use the shared-criteria filter and paired-gap chart for comparisons on the same work. The paired gap is the mean absolute difference between criterion percentages, weighted equally per matched criterion.

Agreement within 10 pp is the proportion of all matched lecturer-pair ratings whose difference is at most 10 percentage points. This fixed band describes proximity, not grading quality. Typical shared-criterion spread is the median of the highest minus lowest rating percentage on each target and criterion scored by at least two selected lecturers. Each target–criterion combination counts once, even when three or more lecturers rated it. Box-plot quartiles use linear interpolation.
